The problem

The invisible job everyone does

Rent, the dentist, emails, groceries, the bill that's due on Friday. We carry all of it in our heads, and nobody sees that work.

Every pending task has three steps. First you have to remember it, then decide what goes first, then get it done. Today's apps only help with the last step.

The technology

Built on multi-agent systems

A multi-agent system is made of several AI agents that work together to carry out tasks on behalf of a user or another system. Each agent keeps its autonomy but cooperates and coordinates with the others. (IBM, Gutowska, 2024)

Each agent usually has a large language model at its core. It can break a complex task into smaller goals, use external tools and keep information in memory. (Guo et al., 2024, IJCAI)

They communicate

Directly with each other, or through the environment they share.

They are orchestrated

An arbiter agent assigns tasks by what each one does best. In Colmena, that's the Queen Bee. (AWS)

They specialize

Knowledge agents, decision agents and execution agents. (AWS)

Where the innovation is. Not in inventing each function, but in bringing them together in one multi-agent system, with a single coordinator and the user's approval on every action.

Why now

Ready to build with, not yet in everyday use

  • 2024Gartner's Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies places multi-agent systems at the innovation trigger, 5 to 10 years from the plateau.
  • 2025The name changes: "AI agents" appear at the peak of inflated expectations, with Gartner noting doubts about their accuracy.
  • 2026Gartner publishes a Hype Cycle just for agentic AI. Multi-agent systems are back by name, inside the peak.

Each year uses a different category and, in 2026, a different Hype Cycle, so the markers aren't one technology moving along one curve. Our reading: the technology is rising fast, hasn't fallen into the trough and isn't mature yet. That's the moment to build Colmena.

Risks and challenges

What could go wrong, and how we handle it

One agent's mistake can spread

In a multi-agent system, an error by one agent can propagate to the others (Guo et al., 2024). Colmena asks for your approval on every action, so nothing goes out on its own.

The technology is still maturing

Gartner has flagged doubts about the accuracy of AI agents. We start with everyday tasks where a person reviews the result before it counts.

Trust and privacy

Colmena handles personal information like emails, payments and appointments. Every connection to your accounts is your decision, and you can remove agents at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Will Colmena send or pay things without asking me?

No. Nothing is sent, paid or confirmed without your approval. Colmena prepares everything, and you give the final OK.

Do I need to learn how to use it?

No. You talk to Colmena the way you'd talk to someone you trust. There are no menus or settings to learn.

What is the Queen Bee?

She is your single point of contact. She understands what you say, sets priorities and hands each part to the right specialist agent. In multi-agent terms, she's the arbiter agent.

How is this different from a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant is a single agent that suggests and waits for permission. Colmena is a team of agents, each with its own role, sharing the work and running tasks at the same time.

Can I add more agents?

Yes. Colmena grows with you. If your life gets more complicated, you add agents for what you need.

Does it replace my apps?

No. It coordinates the apps you already use so they finally work together, from one single place.
